Quantum Node — product brief for NotebookLM video

App name: Quantum Node
Tagline: AI equity terminal
URL: https://stocktrend-ai-super.web.app

One-sentence pitch:
Quantum Node is a private AI-powered stock analysis terminal that helps investors research equities with AI scores, charts, news summaries, valuation context, and trade management zones — behind a secure Google sign-in and subscription.

What it is:
Quantum Node is an AI-assisted market analysis web app (not a broker and not financial advice). After signing in with Google and subscribing, users open a dashboard to analyse stocks with live quotes, AI insights powered by Google Gemini, and decision-support visuals.

Core features to show in the video:
1. Secure access — Google sign-in gate; charts, news, and AI stay private.
2. AI Quantum Stock Score — company name, live price, and an AI score with Buy / Hold / Sell style guidance.
3. Trade Management Zones — BUY → ADD → HOLD → TAKE PROFIT journey for holders already in a position, plus a protective Stop Loss Below (not an entry signal).
4. Historical PE Valuation — PE multiples vs the stock’s own average to frame valuation.
5. AI news summaries — daily news digest for the selected ticker.
6. Market pulse / charts & alerts — terminal-style dark UI for watchlist research.
7. Plans — Basic RM199/month (10 AI analyses + 10 news/day) and Pro RM349/month (30 + 30), with optional mini reloads and analysis packs.

Suggested 45–60 second video script:
[0–8s] Open on the Quantum Node landing page. Narrate: “Quantum Node is an AI equity terminal for serious stock research.”
[8–18s] Show Google sign-in. Narrate: “Sign in securely and unlock your private dashboard.”
[18–30s] Show AI Quantum Stock Score. Narrate: “Get an AI stock score, live price, and clear directional guidance.”
[30–42s] Show Trade Management Zones. Narrate: “Map Buy, Add, Hold, and Take Profit zones — plus a stop-loss floor for risk control.”
[42–52s] Show Historical PE Valuation. Narrate: “Compare today’s PE to the stock’s own history to spot rich or cheap valuation.”
[52–60s] Close on pricing / brand. Narrate: “Basic or Pro plans. Analysis tool only — not financial advice. Quantum Node.”

Risk disclaimer (must include):
